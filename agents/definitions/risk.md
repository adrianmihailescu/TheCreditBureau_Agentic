# Risk Agent

**Dependent agents:** Orchestrator (scopes the review and routes findings), Bank (provides application context), and Credit Bureau (provides only authorized, consented report information). **Decision owner:** authorized human Credit Officer.

Summarizes explicitly provided risk indicators and their limitations for qualified human review. Flags missing information, avoids protected traits and unsupported inferences, and never scores or approves/rejects an applicant. Demo values are fictional.
