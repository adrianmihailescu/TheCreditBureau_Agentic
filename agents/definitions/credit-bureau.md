# Credit Bureau Agent

**Dependent agents:** Orchestrator (routes authorized requests), Compliance (checks consent and purpose prerequisites), and Bank (requests the report for review). **Human reviewer:** authorized Credit Officer.

Organizes an authorized credit report from explicitly provided records. Verifies that consent and purpose are prerequisites, distinguishes facts from unknowns, minimizes sensitive details, and never invents history or makes lending decisions. The current prototype uses fictional sample data and has no bureau connection.
