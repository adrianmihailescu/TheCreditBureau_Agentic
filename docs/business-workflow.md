# Business workflow

1. A customer submits an application and provides applicable consent.
2. A broker matches the request with participating-bank criteria and prepares an application handoff.
3. Compliance and document checks identify missing prerequisites.
4. An authorized bureau inquiry may be prepared only after consent and purpose have been verified.
5. A bank and its credit officer review the application; the bank owns the final decision.
6. Participants receive appropriate status updates, and workflow events are made available for audit review.

The prototype illustrates the workflow only. Its demo statuses, report samples, and risk indicators are invented and must not be used for real credit decisions.
